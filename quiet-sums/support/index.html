Questions

Before you change devices, export a backup file from Settings, even if you rely on your phone’s own backup. Keep it until you’ve checked that all your data is on the new device.

How do I move to a new phone?

If you use iCloud Backup or Google backup, restoring your new phone from that backup brings Quiet Sums back with your data. You can also export a backup file from Settings in Quiet Sums, then choose Replace with backup in Settings on the new phone.

Can I move from iPhone to Android, or back?

Yes, with a backup file. Export it from Settings on the old phone, move the file to the new one however you like (email, cloud storage, a cable), then choose Replace with backup in Settings. Both versions of the app read the same file.

Why does it work on one device at a time?

Quiet Sums has no account and no server, so nothing keeps two devices in sync. When you change phones, the phone’s own backup or transfer brings your data along. A backup file is the fallback, and the way between iPhone and Android.

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset all data in Settings, or uninstall the app. Delete any backup or CSV files you exported, and remove the app’s data from your iCloud or Google backups if you want it gone from there too. Nothing is kept on my side.

How Quiet Sums handles your data is explained in the privacy policy.
