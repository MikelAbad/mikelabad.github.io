In short

Quiet Sums doesn’t collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. What you enter stays on your device.

Who is responsible

Mikel Abad, the independent developer of Quiet Sums. Contact: support@mikelabad.com.

Your data in the app

Your transactions, categories and settings are stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage, protected by the operating system. The app doesn’t send them anywhere, and nobody else receives them, so no third party handles them. I can’t see them.

Backups and moving to a new phone

If you have iCloud Backup or Google backup turned on, your data travels with that backup, under Apple’s or Google’s terms and your own account settings. The backup and CSV files you export from the app go wherever you choose to put them, and are yours to look after.

Deleting your data

Use Reset all data in Settings, or uninstall the app. Delete any exported files, and remove the app’s data from your iCloud or Google backups if you want it gone from there too. I hold none of it, so there is nothing to request from me.

Store statistics

Apple and Google give developers aggregated figures about downloads, ratings and app stability, and crash reports from people who agreed to share them. None of this includes what you enter in the app. If the app asks for a rating, the App Store or Google Play handles it.

If you email me

I receive your email address, your message and whatever you choose to include, plus the app version and platform when you write from the app. Apple hosts the mailbox (iCloud Mail). I use this only to answer you, I never share or sell it, and I keep it only as long as it’s useful for helping you. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you, or object to its use, at support@mikelabad.com. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the copy inside the app together, with a new effective date.

Need help with the app? See Quiet Sums support.
